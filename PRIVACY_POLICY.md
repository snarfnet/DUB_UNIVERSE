# DUB UNIVERSE Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026

DUB UNIVERSE is a music generation app for creating dub techno ideas.

## Audio and Generated Content

Audio files selected in the app are used on the device for analysis and generation. DUB UNIVERSE does not upload your audio files to our servers.

Generated musical patterns and exported MIDI files are created on your device.

## Advertising

DUB UNIVERSE uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google may collect device identifiers, app activity, approximate location, diagnostics, and related data to provide, measure, and improve ads.

Google's privacy policy is available here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data We Collect Directly

We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page:
