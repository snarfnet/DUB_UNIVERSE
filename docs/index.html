DUB UNIVERSE

DUB TECHNO GENERATOR

ABOUT

DUB UNIVERSE analyzes your audio files and generates dub techno patterns based on detected BPM, bass intensity, and atmospheric content. All processing happens on your device.

HOW TO USE

  • Tap "SELECT AUDIO FILE" to load an MP3, WAV, or AIFF file
  • Tap "START ANALYSIS" to detect BPM and audio characteristics
  • Tap "GENERATE DUB TECHNO" to create patterns from your analysis
  • Use mood presets, macro controls, and XY pad to shape the sound
  • Export patterns as MIDI files for use in your DAW

FAQ

Q: What audio formats are supported?
A: MP3, WAV, and AIFF files.

Q: Does the app upload my audio?
A: No. All analysis and generation happens locally on your device.

Q: Can I use generated MIDI in my DAW?
A: Yes. Export MIDI and import it into any DAW that supports standard MIDI files.
